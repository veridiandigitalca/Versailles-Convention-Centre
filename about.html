About Versailles

A Mississauga destination built for meaningful gatherings.

Established in 2007, Versailles Convention Centre has been designed around the scale, hospitality and production needs of memorable events.

Our Approach

Hospitality with a sense of occasion.

Versailles brings together grand architecture, adaptable rooms, in-house culinary service and professional event technology under one roof.

Located in Mississauga, the venue is minutes from Toronto Pearson International Airport and downtown Toronto, making it practical for local and out-of-town guests alike.
