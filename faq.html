FAQ

The useful details, in one place.

Answers to common questions about the venue, events, capacity, menus and planning.

Where is Versailles Convention Centre?

6721 Edwards Blvd, Mississauga, Ontario L5T 2V9.

How large is the venue?

The venue is approximately 22,000 sq ft and includes four grand function rooms.

How many guests can you host?

Capacity depends on the room and configuration. Supplied setup figures indicate approximately 1,375 seated for a dinner wedding and up to approximately 2,875 standing for a cocktail reception. Confirm your specific layout with the venue.

Do you offer South Asian and Halal menus?

Yes. The culinary offering includes Western, Halal, Gujarati, South Asian and Southeast Asian specialties, subject to current menu availability.

Is parking available?

A dedicated surface parking lot is located at the rear of the venue, with free parking.

Is the venue accessible?

Wheelchair-accessible parking and restroom facilities are available.

How do I inquire?

Call +1 (905) 565-7400, email info@versaillesconventioncentre.com, or use the event inquiry form.
